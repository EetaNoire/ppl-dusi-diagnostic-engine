Critical Lab Interpretation Notes

B12 / MMA / Homocysteine

⚠️ Serum B12 in the indeterminate range (180–350 ng/L) does not rule out deficiency.
Test: total or active B12 (holotranscobalamin) first. If the result is indeterminate, MMA is the second-line test, judged against the local laboratory range. Homocysteine: treat according to national guidance.
Always check B12 status before folate supplementation.

Magnesium

⚠️ Serum magnesium is tightly regulated, so a normal serum level does not exclude low body stores. No well-validated test of body stores exists.
Red-cell magnesium is investigational (Table 5, Level C): research use only, not a routine screening test. It should not delay or change a psychiatric diagnosis.

Copper

⚠️ Ceruloplasmin is an acute-phase reactant, elevated by oestrogen, inflammation, pregnancy.
Unreliable as standalone marker. Serum copper + 24-hour urine copper (with contamination precautions) required.
Deficiency risk: post-bariatric surgery, TPN, excess zinc, cystic fibrosis.
Excess: Wilson's disease, OCP, carbamazepine, phenobarbital.

The "Low-Normal" Trap

Serum B12 in the indeterminate range is not reassuring. MMA is the second-line test, judged against the local laboratory range.
Check MMA and homocysteine, not serum B12 alone.

Comorbidity Decision Algorithm

ℹ️ The most common real-world scenario is genuine psychiatric–somatic comorbidity: a patient who has BOTH major depression AND hypothyroidism, BOTH schizophrenia AND B12 deficiency.

Select Applicable Scenario:

SEQUENCING ALGORITHM:

Scenario 1: FESS positive + Psychiatric criteria met:
→ Treat somatic cause FIRST.
→ Reassess psychiatric symptoms after 4–6 weeks of adequate somatic treatment.
→ Assign psychiatric diagnosis ONLY if symptoms persist after somatic correction.
→ This is standard endocrine practice for hypothyroidism presenting with depression.

Scenario 2: FESS positive + Psychiatric emergency:
→ Treat BOTH concurrently.
→ Initiate necessary psychiatric interventions (e.g., antipsychotics for acute psychosis with imminent danger).
→ Simultaneously correct the somatic cause.
→ Document BOTH diagnoses as PROVISIONAL.

Scenario 3: FESS negative + Psychiatric criteria met:
→ Assign psychiatric diagnosis with CONFIDENCE.
→ Somatic mimics have been excluded.
→ This STRENGTHENS the validity of the psychiatric diagnosis.

Four Pathways Checked

01 · Cognitive Bias: Premature closure, anchoring, administrative triage under pressure.
02 · Misattribution: Nutrient, endocrine, infectious, autoimmune, medication mimics.
03 · Uninterrupted Exposure: Causative medication or toxin continues unchecked.
04 · Pharmacologic Masking: Psychiatric treatment suppresses symptoms while somatic illness progresses.
Protocol Goal: Convert diagnostic humility from an individual virtue into a structural requirement.
